Space Router Settings

Rules let you choose which space a site opens in. For example, you can send every facebook.com link straight to your Personal space without being asked. Press R or S in the picker to create one.

About

Zen Browser 1.21.16b and newer ships its own Space Routing (Settings → Spaces). It moves tabs to a space silently, without asking, and runs before this extension sees the tab.

To avoid a tab being routed twice, do not create native routes for domains that have a rule here, and keep Zen's "Default route for external links" on "Most recent Space". This extension cannot read or write Zen's native routes.
